## Design Context

### Users
The owner of Dispatch creates and supervises agent workflows for software, research, and design. This v2 iteration prioritises testing the complete flow on a phone, with desktop editing also supported.

### Brand Personality
Practical, quiet, precise. Keep the original frontend intact; evolve its paper palette into a readable workflow studio on a separate branch.

### Aesthetic Direction
Retain the familiar light paper colour direction from v1. Remove scanline texture, increase readable text sizes, and give work and evidence more room. Use clear operational labels and restrained green status accents.

### Design Principles
- Separate ticket status from execution stages.
- Every step has instructions, a pass condition, a failure route, and an attempt limit.
- Mobile uses a vertical stage sequence and a reachable editor; desktop adds a graph rail and sidebar.
- Test-mode evidence must never imply real verification or provider execution.
- Creating a ticket creates an independent, editable graph.
